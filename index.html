Touch

  • Left half: touch and drag to move. Where you first touch becomes the centre; the direction you drag picks one of 8 arrow-key directions.
  • Snap the aim: the centre stays put for a moment after you lift, so tap the same direction twice quickly to snap the arrow's aim.
  • Right half: top third = special, middle = weight, bottom = grapple. Touching the line between two thirds presses both.

Shortcuts

  • Esc open / close this menu (it also closes the special-ability list first)
  • R restart the match
  • T switch between 1v1 and 2v2
  • 1 2 3 4 toggle AI for Blue, Red, Blue 2, Red 2 (3 and 4 only exist in 2v2)
  • M music on / off
  • On a pick screen: arrows move, grapple or special locks in, weight takes it back

Tips

  • Click the special keycap beside Blue's or Red's goal to pick a different special ability.
  • Online, both the Blue and the Red layout above control your seat.
